# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Saymore serves people who type throughout the working day: developers writing prompts, issues, reviews, and technical documents; product and operations teams writing requirements, email, and customer messages; independent developers who value privacy and provider choice; and Chinese-first knowledge workers who regularly mix Chinese and English.

## Product Purpose

Saymore is a local-first, provider-agnostic, source-available voice typing tool for macOS and Windows. A user places the cursor in any text field, triggers dictation, speaks naturally, and receives accurate, structured text at that cursor. The website should make this workflow immediately understandable and convert qualified visitors toward a verified download or the GitHub repository.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and trustworthy. Saymore should feel like a dependable desktop utility with visible technical boundaries, not a conversational agent that makes broad promises.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS landing pages built from repeated rounded cards, purple gradients, and inflated claims.
- Voice-agent marketing that implies screen reading, reply generation, task execution, or automatic sending.
- A visual clone of Typeless, Shandianshuo, Type-X, or any single competitor.
- Fabricated performance metrics, app compatibility claims, testimonials, pricing, or product availability.
- Calling Saymore open source. It is source-available under PolyForm Shield 1.0.0.

## Design Principles

1. Show the complete dictation path before describing it.
2. Make data boundaries and provider choice visible at the moment they matter.
3. Prefer measured evidence over category claims.
4. Preserve the user's words and the product's honest scope.
5. Treat download trust, recovery behavior, and licensing as product features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All interactions must be keyboard accessible, focus must remain visible, color cannot be the only state indicator, body text must meet 4.5:1 contrast, and motion must provide a reduced-motion alternative. Chinese and English text must remain legible without clipped labels or forced single-line layouts.
